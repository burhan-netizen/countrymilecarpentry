The rough-sawn Jarrah staircase

Old 75x300 rough-sawn Jarrah, kept rough through every part of the stair, under a beamed ceiling.

Plan a build like this Call Daniel
JarrahEucalyptus marginata
“Was a pleasure creating this staircase with this old 75x300 rough sawn Jarrah. They wanted to keep the rough-sawn look through all the components and it turned out epic!”Daniel Weston, on Instagram

The story

The owners had old, heavy Jarrah and a clear idea: keep the rough-sawn character everywhere. Not just the treads, but the stringers, the newels, the handrail. Most stairs get planed smooth. This one was built to look like it had always been part of the house.

Working with big old sections means reading every board. Daniel matched the timber to the old beams and brick in the room, so the stair sits in the space rather than on top of it.
