The pine tree retreat

A Jarrah deck under an old pine, a pavilion for the sauna, and a sunken fire pit to finish the night.

Plan a build like this Call Daniel
JarrahEucalyptus marginata
“Jarrah deck beneath the shade of this pine tree for a sauna and outdoor shower to follow.”Daniel Weston, on Instagram

The story

The brief was simple to say and hard to build: an outdoor retreat that sits under a big old pine without hurting it. Daniel framed the deck around the trunk, left the tree room to move with an octagon cut-out trimmed in Jarrah, and picture-framed every edge with mitred boards.

Then the deck grew. A Jarrah pavilion went up to shelter the barrel sauna, and a second run of decking steps down into a sunken fire pit with seating on three sides. It's one connected place now, not three separate jobs.

Part 1 of 3

The deck around the pine

Framing first, then decking that wraps the trunk. The octagon trim gives the tree room to grow and makes the cut-out look deliberate.

Part 2 of 3

The sauna pavilion

A skillion roof on Jarrah posts and rafters, built to house the barrel sauna and keep the weather off it.

Part 3 of 3

The sunken fire pit

Decking that steps down into a gravel floor, with seating built into three sides and a cast fire bowl in the middle.
