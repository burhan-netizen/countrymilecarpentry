The Karri staircase

Every tread housed and checked into the next, built in the shed, then suspended off a limestone wall.

“The Karri stair case is finished and I couldn't be happier with how it's come up! Each piece of timber housed and checked into each other and then suspended off the limestone wall was a challenge but such a rewarding end result!”Daniel Weston, on Instagram

The story

Stairs are where carpentry shows. There's nowhere to hide a gap on a staircase you walk past every day, and this one had to hang off a limestone wall with skylights pouring light straight onto it.

Daniel marked out and housed every tread by hand, then built the whole staircase in his shed first so every joint was right before it went near the house. Only then did it go to site, get suspended off the stone, and finished with a Karri handrail and steel balusters.
