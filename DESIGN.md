# Country Mile Carpentry: design system (locked)

Direction: timber journal. Each job is told like a feature story, in Daniel's own words, with the species of WA timber shown on a yard-style timber tag.

## Colour
- Limestone (page): #EEE8DE, from the Witchcliffe limestone wall on the Karri stair
- Paper card: #F8F4EE
- Charred Jarrah ink: #24160F (text, logo black)
- Muted ink: #6A5A4F (labels, second headline line)
- Jarrah heartwood accent: #8E3118 (primary buttons, final CTA, active states). One accent only.
- Dark band: #1D1410

## Type
- Newsreader (variable, optical size): headlines, pull quotes, botanical names in italic
- Hanken Grotesk: body, UI, tags
- No mono, no all caps labels (logo excepted)

## Signature
- Timber tag: a punched yard tag (common name + botanical name) pinned on photos and project heads
- Timber index: pick a species, see its grain and every job built from it
- Shed to site: sticky photo story of the Karri stair, which doubles as the 4-step process

## Rules
- Buttons: pill. Cards and photos: 22px radius, large blocks 32px.
- Section labels: small serif italic, sentence case
- No em dashes anywhere
